
# PrepSaathi AI

### Your Personal AI Learning & Exam Preparation Partner

PrepSaathi AI is a multilingual AI-powered learning platform designed to help students prepare for competitive examinations and school education.

## Our Languages
- English
- Bengali
- Hindi

## Competitive Examination Preparation
- UPSC
- WBCS
- SSC
- Banking

## School Education
- CBSE
- CISCE (ICSE and ISC)
- West Bengal Board (WBBSE and WBCHSE)

## Our Mission

To make personalised, accessible and affordable AI-powered education available to every student.

## Technology

This project will be developed using modern web technologies, a secure database and AI-powered learning tools.
